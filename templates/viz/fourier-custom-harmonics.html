Custom Harmonics Builder — Additive Synthesis

Additive synthesis (each row = one harmonic)

\[ y(t) = \sum_{i=1}^{K} A_{i}\,\sin\!\big(2\pi (n_{i}\, f_{0})\, t + \varphi_{i}\big) \]

Ai harmonic amplitude ni integer multiple of fundamental φi phase offset

Reference: Wikipedia — Additive synthesis; Roads, C. (1996). The Computer Music Tutorial, MIT Press, Ch. 4. This is the technique used inside pipe-organ stops and the Hammond drawbar organ.

Each row is one sine wave. Add rows and tune them — this is the most "hands-on" way to feel why a clarinet (mostly odd harmonics) sounds different from a violin (rich in all harmonics) even when both play the same pitch.
